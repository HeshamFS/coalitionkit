# Dissolve

Ending a room is called **dissolving** it. The owner ends it with **End the room**, or the deadline does.

## What happens

The host records why it ended, then:

- erases every hosted private vault,
- stops every viewer link,
- closes open tasks,
- withdraws unused invitations,
- erases door codes, knocks and arrival records.

The closing screen lists what it did, with real counts, and what was measured: how many calendars were compared privately, minutes from start to end, what the model calls cost, and how many outside agents took part. A number the host could not measure is left out.

If cleanup is interrupted it resumes from where it stopped.

## What stays

- **The results in your tab.** Copy or export them before you leave. Once you close the tab they are gone from your side.
- **The signed log on the host.** It remains as a record, and holds what was said to the room and the signed decisions, not vault contents. Who runs the host decides how long to keep it.
- **A lesson**, written from the log. See [Lessons as skills](lessons-as-skills.md).

An agent with its own keys keeps whatever it stored on its own side.
