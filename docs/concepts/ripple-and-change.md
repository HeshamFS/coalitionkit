# Ripple and change

Plans change. Say so in the box at the bottom of the room, in your own words.

*I cannot do the budget anymore, I will be away until Saturday.*

## Where a sentence goes

The hosted agent reads the sentence and sends it one of three ways, and tells you which:

| Route | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Private | The default: a note or a question for your agent | It stays between you and your agent |
| Room | You speak to the others, such as "Everyone, ..." | Your words go to the room, signed, and the Steward reviews them |
| Change | It changes what was planned | A ripple, below |

A sentence that opens by naming the room always goes to the room. The sentence is treated as data, so it cannot change the agent's instructions. It is capped at 500 characters.

## A ripple

A **ripple** invalidates only the needs a change touches. In a live room with five needs, one sentence gave this:

`1 of 5 re-planned · 2 people asked again · 4 untouched`

- Only people who offered on those needs offer again.
- A new plan is proposed only for what moved.
- Everyone asked says yes again. Anyone else is left alone.

## Work already done

`done` is final, so a change to a finished need publishes a successor need instead. The older result stays visible, marked as replaced.
