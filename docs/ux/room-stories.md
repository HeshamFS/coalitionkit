# What people and agents do in a room

Every story from entering a room to leaving it. `docs/ux/room-engine.md` holds the contract that delivers them. "Before" is what a real web room did on 2026-11-03 when these stories were written.

| # | Story | You | Agents | You see | Before |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | Enter and see who is here | Walk in from the Door | Your agent takes its seat with you | The ring of seats, filled and open | Partly |
| R-02 | Agree to the goal | Hold once to take your seat | Nothing until you sign | What you agree to, what stays private | Works |
| R-03 | See the goal become real needs | Watch, or correct one | The Gardener splits the goal with the model | Needs arriving as labelled skeletons, then filling | Partly |
| R-04 | Brief my agent, privately | Answer up to three questions | Your agent picks what to ask and which component to ask with | A calendar, a slider or choices made for you; others see nothing | Missing |
| R-05 | Bring my own agent | Connect by MCP, A2A or a message door | Your agent acts under the same rules | Its vendor on your seat | Works |
| R-06 | Watch agents offer | Nothing; you can leave | Each agent reads the needs and its vault, then offers what its person can do | Beams from seats to needs; your agent's private feed on your side | Missing |
| R-07 | Find what fits, without telling | Nothing | Vaults compare times through blinded rounds | Points crossing through the host, then "your other N stayed private" | Missing |
| R-08 | See the Steward stop something | Nothing; it already happened | The Steward blocks a message that breaks a rule | The message stopped in mid-air, with the rule named | Partly |
| R-09 | Compare plans | Pick one | The Gardener builds plans by rule; the model writes what each means for you | Plans side by side, with a "for you only" line on each | Partly |
| R-10 | Sign what commits me | Hold to sign | Agents cannot do this for you | If you say yes, what stays private, the receipt | Works |
| R-11 | Agents make the real things | Open, flip, export | Agents build budgets, schedules, lists and drafts | Results growing on the table, each with "show the work" | Missing |
| R-12 | Do my part | Mark done, or approve a draft | Your agent commits only after your yes, and prepares what it can | Only your tasks, on your side | Missing |
| R-13 | Talk to my agent or the room | Say anything in one box | Your agent decides if it stays private or goes to the room, and tells you which | Your private feed, or a change on the table | Missing |
| R-14 | Something changes | Say it, or answer one question | The ripple re-plans only what it touches | A wave, the affected needs moving, the rest still, with real counts | Missing |
| R-15 | Be absent, or arrive late | Nothing, or catch up in a few lines | Your agent answers from your vault and waits for your yes on commitments | A short "while you were away" | Missing |
| R-16 | Add a business or a machine | Paste a card, or mint from a video | The host checks the card and seats it | A square seat with only its orders | Partly |
| R-17 | See it as someone else | Tap a seat | The host composes the view for both your scopes | Their screen, with what is private to them hatched out | Partly |
| R-18 | See how a screen was made | Flip any result | Nothing new; it shows what the model chose | The component tree, its events and the check it passed | Missing |
| R-19 | Check the record myself | Press check | Nothing; your browser does the math | Signed links lighting up, or the first broken one in red | Works |
| R-20 | End it and keep what matters | End the room, or let the deadline do it | Lessons become skills; vaults and links are erased | The ring folds into the mark; results stay, each claim with its count | Partly |
| R-21 | Take part from a phone | One thing at a time | Same | Your side first, the table one swipe away | Partly |

If time runs short, keep R-01 to R-11 and R-14. Cut R-15, R-16 and phone polish first.
