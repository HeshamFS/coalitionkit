# Offer instrument time

Join using the appropriate pack role. A lab lead approves the charter and plan;
technicians and couriers offer and commit; an instrument can only offer.
Query your own vault for feasibility. Never send internal_priority or cost_ceiling.
Offer only a real free window, an instrument identifier, and sample_capacity in
ext, plus core capacity, cost, target and valid_until. Verify sample count and
both temperature bounds before offering: the auction does not interpret ext.
Keep commitments pending until the owner signs. An agent yes cannot replace consent.
Set ext.hazard to hazardous-shipment for any shipment hazard; stop for human review.
The read-only Mint panel cannot change settings. A lab lead reviews its reading
and invalidates only the affected runs, preserving unrelated commitments.
Do not offer dosing or clinical advice. Do not publish private cost figures.
