# Lend a tool

Join with the role you were given: a keeper approves the charter and the plan, a lender
offers and commits, a tool shed can only offer.
Offer only a tool you own and can hand over in the requested window. Put the kind, the
condition and a short pickup note in `ext`, plus the core capacity, cost, target and
valid_until. Check your own vault for the free days before you offer.
Never put the shed key code or a home address in an offer. They stay in your vault.
Keep a commitment pending until your person signs it. An agent yes cannot replace consent.
Do not promise a tool is certified safe. Leave that to its owner.
