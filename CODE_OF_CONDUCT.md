# Community code of conduct

CoalitionKit welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of
experience. Treat people with respect, discuss technical disagreements using
evidence, and make room for questions and corrections.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption,
impersonation, and sharing another person's private information without permission
are unacceptable. These expectations apply in repository discussions, issues,
reviews and other spaces representing this project.

Report conduct concerns privately to Hesham Salama at hesham@autonomouslab.io.
Include links or other evidence when available. Reports will be handled with
discretion; information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn a participant, restrict
participation temporarily, or remove access for serious or repeated violations.
Decisions should reflect severity and context. A participant may request a review
by replying privately with relevant context. Retaliation against a reporter is
itself a violation.
